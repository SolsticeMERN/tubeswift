Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TubeSwift does not require user registration, login credentials, credit card details, or personal identity information. We do not maintain server records linking your IP address to specific media downloads.

2. Log Files and Local Storage

Like standard web servers, we may automatically collect anonymous diagnostic telemetry such as browser type, referring URLs, operating system, and timestamp solely for server maintenance and DDOS prevention. Download history is stored exclusively on your device's browser (HTML5 localStorage) and is never transmitted to our backend.

3. Cookies & Advertising Partners

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Page.

4. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to browse without tracking. Because we collect zero personally identifiable information, we do not sell or trade user data.

5. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to revise this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
