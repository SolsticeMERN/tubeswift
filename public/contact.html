DMCA Takedown Procedure

TubeSwift operates as a transient client-side proxy tool. We do not host, store, index, or maintain third-party audiovisual works. However, in compliance with 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), if you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe content accessible through our utility infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a formal notification containing:

  • A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the specific URL(s) you request to be blocked or restricted.
  • Contact information including your name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is unauthorized.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.
